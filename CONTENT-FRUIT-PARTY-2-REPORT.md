# Content expansion log — Fruit Party 2

Date: 2026-11-02  
Page: `/slots/fruit-party-2/`  
Locales: de, en, pl, es, pt, ja.

## What changed

The six localized profile pages now explain the title-specific distinction between the base game and Free Spins:

- Cluster wins and the tumble sequence in the base game.
- Wild multipliers in the base game that can add across winning clusters; the provider announcement states up to 256×.
- Three or more Scatters in one spin trigger Free Spins.
- Random Wilds continue during Free Spins; the provider announcement lists up to 729× on an individual Wild.
- Three or more Golden Fruit Scatters retrigger the bonus.
- A practical checklist for following clusters, tumbles, Wilds and Scatter conditions.

The two multiplier figures are explicitly described as different rules and units. The text does not imply that the 729× individual-Wild value is the same as the 256× base-game combined multiplier.

Existing URLs, translation keys, author metadata, CTA, related links and catalogue navigation are preserved. `lastmod` reflects the actual content change; review status remains `editorial-review-pending`.

No RTP, volatility, stake limits, maximum-win figure or personal test-session claim was added.

## Source

Pragmatic Play, [Fruit Party 2 announcement dated 5 August 2021](https://www.pragmaticplay.com/en/news/pragmatic-play-back-with-juicier-wins-than-ever-in-fruit-party-2/). It describes base-game Wild multipliers that can add across winning clusters up to 256×; three or more Scatters triggering Free Spins; individual random Wild multipliers up to 729× during the feature; and three or more Golden Fruit Scatters retriggering the bonus.

The provider announcement is a dated description of the game mechanics, not a substitute for the help and paytable of the version currently loaded by an operator.
