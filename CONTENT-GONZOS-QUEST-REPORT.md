## What changed

The six localized pages now explain the original Gonzo's Quest mechanics:

- Five reels, three rows and 20 fixed paylines.
- Avalanche: winning symbols disappear, symbols fall into empty positions, and successive Avalanches can create additional evaluations in one paid spin.
- The base-game multiplier grows during consecutive Avalanches, with NetEnt listing a maximum of x5.
- Wild symbols substitute for regular symbols; NetEnt's product page says Wilds can also stand in for Free Fall Scatters.
- Three Free Fall Scatters on reels 1, 2 and 3 trigger ten Free Falls.
- Free Falls use higher Avalanche multiplier levels, up to x15.
- A documented operator help page gives a step sequence for the multipliers and the left-to-right Scatter condition. These details are explicitly attributed to that operator version, not presented as a universal NetEnt configuration.
- The original Gonzo's Quest is kept separate from Gonzo's Quest II.

Existing URLs, translation keys, author metadata, CTA, related links and catalogue navigation are preserved. Localized catalogue descriptions were updated and the German image alt text grammar error corrected. `lastmod` reflects the content change; review status remains `editorial-review-pending`.

No RTP, stake range, volatility or universal operator configuration has been added.
