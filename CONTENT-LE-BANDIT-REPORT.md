## What changed

The six localized profile pages now explain Le Bandit's multi-stage feature system:

- Six-reel, five-row Cluster Pays layout.
- Super Cascades: contributing symbols and matching visible symbols disappear; new symbols fall into place while wins continue.
- Golden Squares preserve winning positions for later activation by a Rainbow symbol.
- Bronze, Silver and Gold Coin ranges as listed by Hacksaw Gaming.
- Four-Leaf Clovers multiply adjacent cells; Pots of Gold collect visible coin values and other Pots in the stated order.
- The three distinct bonus modes and their FS triggers:
  - Luck of the Bandit (3 FS), including the documented retriggers and upgrade path.
  - All That Glitters Is Gold (4 FS, 12 initial spins), with Golden Squares retained through the round.
  - Treasure at the End of the Rainbow (5 FS, 12 spins), with a Rainbow on every spin; the provider says this mode cannot be purchased.
- A practical checklist for following Golden Squares, Rainbow activation, coin collection and bonus transitions.

Existing URLs, translation keys, author metadata, CTA, related links and catalogue navigation are preserved. `lastmod` reflects the actual content change; review status remains `editorial-review-pending`.

No RTP, stake range, volatility or maximum-win figure was added as a universal claim, despite values being listed on the provider page. The exact version and operator configuration must still be checked in the game's own help.
