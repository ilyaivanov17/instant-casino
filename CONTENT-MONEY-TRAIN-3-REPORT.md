## What changed

The six localized profile pages now explain the specific features described in Relax Gaming's product announcement:

- The four returning characters: Collector, Sniper, Necromancer and Collector-Payer.
- Persistent Shapeshifter: changes into a feature on each spin.
- Absorber: collects multipliers and adds them to its own value.
- Tommy Gun: held by Payer or Sniper, targets a symbol type and increases its value.
- Money Cart Bonus Round: three or more Bonus symbols anywhere on the reels trigger the round; triggering symbols reveal stake-multiple values and three spins are awarded.
- Respins: can begin after a spin with no win; one existing symbol is randomly held for an additional spin.
- A practical checklist that keeps these mechanics separate instead of treating them as one generic bonus.

The page deliberately does not invent internal trigger details that the release announcement does not disclose. Exact values, feature evaluation and operator configurations should be checked against the help for the loaded game.

Existing URLs, translation keys, author metadata, CTA, related links and catalogue navigation are preserved. `lastmod` reflects the content change; review status remains `editorial-review-pending`.

No RTP, stake range, volatility or maximum-win claim was added as a universal fact, and no personal test session is claimed.
