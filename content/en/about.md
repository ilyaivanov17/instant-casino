## How to prepare a request

Describe the topic, when the issue occurred and what needs attention. Share only information required to investigate the request. For a technical issue, the browser, device, page URL and a screenshot with private information removed are usually useful.

| Request | Helpful details | Do not send |
|---|---|---|
| Content correction | Page URL, exact passage and a reliable supporting source | Passwords or one-time codes |
| Broken link | Page URL, link label and the destination that failed | Login credentials |
| Casino account or payment issue | Contact the operator's official support | Full card details, security codes or passwords |
| Technical issue | Device, browser, time and a screenshot without private data | Other people's data |
| Partnership enquiry | Organisation, topic and business contact | Unnecessary identity documents |

### Protect personal information

Never send passwords, recovery codes, full payment-card data or another person's account details. Share personal information only when it is necessary to handle your request.

# Editorial policy and comparison method

This page explains how Instant Casino organises information about games, bonus offers, payment methods and common questions. The aim is a transparent comparison: promotional headlines are considered alongside the terms that actually affect a decision.

## What we compare

| Topic | Comparison criteria | What readers should check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slots and games | Provider, RTP, volatility, stake range and recorded features | RTP is a long-run theoretical figure, not a prediction for an individual round. Details can vary by game version. |
| Bonuses | Amount, minimum deposit, wagering, expiry, maximum stake and exclusions | The full bonus terms apply; the headline amount alone does not show the real value of an offer. |
| Payments | Method, recorded limits, listed processing time and known restrictions | Availability, fees and withdrawal routes can depend on country, account and method. |
| Operator and licence | Operator name, authority, licence number and primary-source link | A casino operator’s licence is not automatically a licence for an independent information publisher. |

## Sources and changes

Details that may change—especially bonus terms, payment methods, limits and availability—should be checked against the provider’s current information before use. If a catalogue entry does not contain a value, we label it as not listed. That does not mean the value is zero or that no restriction exists.

For operator and licence information, we link to the provider’s official privacy notice. Company details in that notice refer to the casino operator and do not automatically identify the publisher of this information website. Sources need to be read according to their role: primary sources are relevant for current provider terms; a comparison website offers an external perspective but does not replace the provider’s own terms.

## Authors and editorial contribution

Author profiles describe each contributor’s subject focus and link to public professional profiles. A profile helps readers understand the topics an author covers; it is not a substitute for evidence on an individual page. We do not claim to have completed a real-money test unless a documented test result exists. Technical data, editorial assessment and player opinions should remain distinguishable.

## Affiliate disclosure

Some links may lead to a commercial partner. A commission, where one is paid, is a commercial relationship—not evidence that an offer is better or safer. Editorial comparisons should follow the stated criteria and available sources. Readers should review the applicable terms before registration, a deposit or bonus activation.

## Updates and corrections

When material terms change, the relevant page should be updated and its change date refreshed. A correction request is most useful when it includes the URL, the specific statement and a reliable source. Account, deposit and withdrawal issues must be directed to the operator’s official support; this information website does not manage player accounts.
