A withdrawal is not always a single transaction. After you submit a request, the operator may review it, request verification and then pass the payment to a provider, card network or bank. Each stage can have a different timeframe.

This guide explains the process in general terms. It does not promise a payout time or confirm the terms of a particular operator. Instant Casino is an information website; it does not hold player balances or process payments.

## Before you request a withdrawal

Check these points in your account:

- Is identity verification complete, or are documents still requested?
- Is the chosen withdrawal method available for your country and account?
- Do the account and payment-method holder details match where required?
- Is a bonus active, with wagering or other conditions still outstanding?
- Are there minimums, maximums, fees or currency-conversion costs?
- Do the terms distinguish operator processing from the bank or payment-provider transfer?

The current cashier and the operator's applicable terms take precedence over general comparison tables or old user reports.

## The main stages of a payout

### 1. Submit the request
Choose a method displayed in the cashier, enter the amount and review the final summary. Check the currency, recipient details and any fees before confirming.

### 2. Operator review
The operator may review account details, payment ownership, bonus status and any outstanding requirements. A status such as “pending” or “under review” can mean this stage is not complete. Status labels differ between operators.

### 3. Identity or payment verification
An operator may request evidence to confirm identity, age or entitlement to use a payment method. Requirements and timing depend on the case and applicable rules. Upload documents only through the official, secure account area. Never send passwords, one-time codes or full card details to private contacts.

### 4. Payment-provider or bank processing
After approval, the transfer may still need to be processed by a wallet provider, card network or bank. Weekends, public holidays, bank schedules and additional checks can affect arrival. Ask whether the payout has actually been released or is still being reviewed.

## Common reasons for delays

| Possible reason | What to check |
|---|---|
| Verification is incomplete | Look for a specific request in your account. |
| Details do not match | Check the name and payment details against the account requirements. |
| Internal review | Check official messages and the transaction status. |
| Bonus conditions remain | Review wagering, expiry and withdrawal clauses. |
| Provider or bank processing | Ask whether the operator has released the payment. |
| Limits or currency | Check the amount, currency and applicable limits. |

A delay alone does not establish that a payment has been mishandled. Ask support for the current stage and a transaction reference through an official channel.

## Payment methods and withdrawal rules

A method being available for deposits does not mean it is available for withdrawals. Some operators require withdrawals to return through the original payment route or apply additional checks. Others may offer different methods for payouts.

| Method | What to verify |
|---|---|
| Bank card | Whether the issuer supports credits or refunds to the card. |
| E-wallet | Whether the wallet is verified and enabled for withdrawals. |
| Bank transfer | Account-holder details, IBAN, currency and bank fees. |
| Prepaid method | Whether withdrawals are supported or another route is required. |
| Cryptocurrency | Exact coin, network and address, plus network fees and price risk. |

Never use a payment address sent in an unsolicited message. Cryptocurrency transfers may be irreversible.

## Bonuses and pending wagering

An active bonus may affect a withdrawal. Depending on the promotion's terms, requesting a payout can cancel a bonus or remaining progress. Restrictions may also apply to eligible games, deadlines, maximum stakes or cash-out amounts.

Before requesting a payout, check whether a bonus is active, whether wagering is complete and what the terms say about withdrawal. There is no single rule for every promotion. See our [guide to bonus terms]({{< relref "bonuses" >}}).

## What to do if a payout is late

1. Note the date, amount, method and status in your transaction history.
2. Check official account notifications and email for a specific document request.
3. Compare the elapsed time with the current terms for your account.
4. Contact support using the website or app you opened yourself through a known official address.
5. Ask whether the payment is still under review or has been sent to the provider, and request a transaction reference.

Do not pay an alleged release, tax or verification fee to a private person or unknown wallet. Do not make another deposit just because a message claims it will unlock existing funds. Never share passwords or one-time codes.

## Check local authorization

Gambling rules differ by country. Before registering or depositing, check the official regulator's register or whitelist for your jurisdiction and verify the exact domain and product category. A foreign licence or a payment logo alone does not establish that an operator is authorized where you live.

## Responsible play

Gambling is not an income source and a payout is never guaranteed. Only spend what you can afford to lose. Do not chase losses with further deposits. If gambling affects your finances or wellbeing, use available limits, blocking tools and local support services.
