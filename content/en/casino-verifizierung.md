KYC means “Know Your Customer”. It refers to checks an operator may use to establish identity, age or certain payment details. Which checks are required or permitted depends on the jurisdiction, the service and the individual circumstances. This guide is general information, not legal advice. Instant Casino does not operate player accounts or perform identity checks.

## What evidence might be requested?

Depending on the case, an operator may ask for:

- **Identity:** a valid identity document where legally and contractually required;
- **Address:** an appropriate proof of address if it needs to be checked;
- **Payment method:** limited details or evidence that you are entitled to use a card, bank account or wallet;
- **Transaction:** information needed to identify or explain a particular payment, where relevant.

This is not a universal document list. Submit only items specifically requested and check which details must remain visible. Redact information only if the operator explicitly permits it.

## How to submit documents safely

1. Open the known official website yourself and sign in.
2. Read the exact request, including accepted file types and formats.
3. Upload documents only through the secure account area.
4. Keep the submission confirmation.
5. If the request is unclear or inconsistent, ask through an official support channel.

Do not send identity documents, passwords, one-time codes or full card details to private messaging contacts or unknown email addresses. Do not trust unexpected document-upload links without independently verifying them.

## Why a check may take place

Verification may occur when an account is opened, before a withdrawal or when account details need updating. Inconsistent information, a new payment method or a legal requirement may trigger a review. Ask the operator for the specific reason and expected next step.

If a withdrawal is waiting on verification, ask whether documents are missing or already under review. Never pay a private person or unknown wallet an alleged KYC or release fee.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does every casino request the same documents?
No. The scope and timing vary by operator, local rules and individual circumstances.

### Is KYC the same as proof of payment?
Not necessarily. Identity, address and entitlement to use a payment method are separate checks.

### What if verification takes unusually long?
Ask official support for the status, any missing information and a reference. Do not repeatedly send documents through insecure channels.
