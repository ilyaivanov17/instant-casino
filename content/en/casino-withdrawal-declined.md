A declined withdrawal can have several possible causes. These may include incomplete account details, an unsupported payment method, outstanding verification, limits or bonus conditions. The exact reason and appropriate next steps depend on the operator and applicable rules. Instant Casino is an information website; it does not manage player accounts or process payments.

## Identify the actual status first

Open the transaction history and read the notice in your official account. Distinguish between “declined”, “cancelled”, “returned” and “under review”; these labels may have different meanings. If the message gives no clear explanation, contact support through the official website you opened yourself and ask for the reason and a reference.

## Common possible causes

| Possible reason | What to check |
|---|---|
| Verification outstanding | Is there a specific document request in your account? |
| Details do not match | Are your name and payment details accurate and consistent? |
| Method unavailable for withdrawals | Is it explicitly listed in the withdrawal area? |
| Limit exceeded | Is the amount within current minimum and maximum limits? |
| Bonus terms incomplete | Are wagering, deadlines and promotion rules satisfied? |
| Technical or bank issue | Is there an error message or transaction reference? |

These are general possibilities, not a diagnosis of your individual case.

## What to do next

1. Save the date, amount, payment method, status and any error message.
2. Check the current terms and your account for outstanding requirements.
3. Ask support whether the request was finally declined or returned for correction.
4. Request the specific rule or term relied on and whether a new request is permitted.
5. Submit any required evidence only through the secure official account area.

Do not submit another request until you know whether the original one has been closed and how a new request should be made.

## Beware of alleged release fees

Do not pay a private person or unknown wallet an extra fee to unlock a declined withdrawal. Never share passwords, one-time codes or full card details. Verify unusual demands through an independently accessed official contact channel.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does “declined” mean the balance is lost?
Not automatically. The status and consequences depend on the account terms. Ask for a written explanation of what happens to the amount.

### Can I submit a new request immediately?
Only after support confirms that the original request is closed and explains the conditions for a new one.

### What if the operator does not respond?
Keep records of the communication and check the relevant regulator or complaint body in your jurisdiction. First verify whether the operator is authorized there.
