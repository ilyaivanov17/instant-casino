A first withdrawal may be the first time an operator checks account details, identity information and the payment route together. This does not mean every first payout is delayed or that every operator asks for the same documents. Requirements depend on the operator, jurisdiction, payment method and individual circumstances. Instant Casino is an information website; it does not hold player accounts or process payments.

## Before requesting your first withdrawal

Review these points in the official account area:

1. **Account details:** Are your name, date of birth and address accurate and consistent with valid records?
2. **Verification:** Is there an outstanding request relating to identity, age or address?
3. **Payment method:** Is the method explicitly available for withdrawals, not just deposits?
4. **Ownership:** Is the payment method registered to you where the operator requires this?
5. **Bonus terms:** Have wagering requirements, deadlines and other promotion rules been met?
6. **Limits and fees:** Is the amount within the displayed limits, and are any charges clear?

The current cashier and applicable operator terms take precedence over general user reports.

## Why a first payout may involve extra checks

An operator may request additional information before approving a first withdrawal. This could involve identity verification, evidence relating to payment-method ownership or clarification of inconsistent account details. A request for review is not automatically evidence of wrongdoing. Ask what specific information is missing and which secure channel should be used.

## If the request remains pending

Check the transaction history and official account notifications first. Upload requested documents only through the secure account area. Then contact support through the official website you opened yourself. Ask which processing stage is outstanding and request a transaction reference. Avoid submitting multiple overlapping withdrawal requests while the first is being reviewed.

Never pay a private person or unknown wallet an alleged release fee to receive a payout. Do not share passwords or one-time codes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Must KYC be completed before the first withdrawal?
That depends on the operator and applicable rules. Verification may be requested before or during processing.

### Is the first withdrawal always slower?
No universal timeframe applies. Operator review and subsequent bank or payment processing are separate stages.

### Can I use a different method from the one used for deposit?
That depends on the operator's terms and the options available in your account.
