## A record win — and an important distinction

On 11 October 2018, NetEnt announced a €3.5 million jackpot on Mega Fortune. The winner was a player from Lower Saxony who was playing at PokerStars Casino. According to the announcement, he was a journalist who arrived home in the evening and started a session while a pizza was cooking in the oven. NetEnt also said that he had deposited just over €2 before the win. The company described the payout as the largest online win in PokerStars Casino history at that time. It also reported that Mega Fortune had paid out eight jackpots of more than €1 million during 2018.

The story is striking, but one detail matters when explaining the game: **NetEnt reported a deposit of just over €2, not a €2 stake on the winning spin.** The press release does not state the stake used on that spin or how many spins came before it. Describing the event as “a €2 bet turned into €3.5 million” would therefore go beyond what the source confirms.

## What makes a jackpot progressive?

A fixed prize is specified in a paytable. A progressive jackpot, by contrast, grows according to a defined system until it is triggered and paid out. Depending on the game, the pool may be local to one game environment or shared across connected game instances. Its funding model, eligibility rules and reset procedure should be explained in the game rules.

Before playing, a reader should be able to find out:
- how the jackpot is funded and whether it has a seed value or a cap;
- which stakes or activated paylines qualify for the jackpot;
- how it is triggered and what happens after a payout;
- whether the displayed RTP includes the jackpot or reports it separately.

The UK Gambling Commission's technical guidance for progressive jackpots uses these as transparency requirements: rules should explain funding, the starting amount, any ceiling and how prizes are awarded. This is a useful technical reference, not a statement of German law.

## Mega Fortune as a concrete example

NetEnt describes Mega Fortune as a five-reel, three-row slot with 25 paylines. The product page also lists free spins, Scatter symbols, Wild substitutions and a jackpot bonus game with three progressive jackpots: two smaller local jackpots and one global jackpot.

According to the published game description, **three Bonus symbols on an activated payline** trigger the jackpot bonus. The bonus game uses three wheels. The player stops the wheel, and arrows in the bonus sequence lead to further jackpot chances. The feature ends with either a jackpot or a fixed coin prize. The product description does not say that the timing of the click changes the probability of winning. The stop button should not be presented as a skill-based trick or a way to force a jackpot.

NetEnt's public product page currently displays a theoretical RTP of 96.60%. However, it does not provide a clearly attributable volatility category in the accessible product information. We therefore do not assign a label such as “high” or “medium”, and we do not infer jackpot odds from the displayed prize amount. Game versions, regional settings and in-game information may differ.

## RTP: what 96.60% actually means

RTP stands for “return to player”. It is a theoretical percentage calculated over a very large number of game rounds. A displayed RTP of 96.60% does not mean that a player will receive €96.60 back after staking €100 in a short session. An individual session can finish well above or below that figure even when the game operates according to its mathematical design.

For a game with a progressive jackpot, it is also important to understand how the jackpot component is treated in the RTP figure. The Gambling Commission notes that the base game and progressive jackpot may need to be monitored separately and that the RTP presentation should make clear whether one combined figure or separate figures are used.

The public Mega Fortune product page does not make it clear whether the displayed 96.60% combines the base-game and jackpot components or how they are split. For a particular game version, the information shown in the game itself is therefore the relevant reference.

## Volatility: why a million-euro jackpot is not a prediction

Volatility describes how widely and how irregularly results can vary around a game's long-term return. Lower-volatility games tend to produce smaller wins more often; higher-volatility games can produce larger wins less frequently. A progressive jackpot is typically a rare event with a potentially large payout. That fact alone does not establish a volatility category for the entire slot.

A large jackpot display is also not evidence that a win is “due”. The size of the prize and the probability of triggering it are different pieces of information. NetEnt's public product page does not publish a specific probability for the Mega Fortune jackpot. We therefore cannot provide a spin count, “good” stake level or timing method that would make a win more likely.

## What the 2018 story really demonstrates

The 2018 case illustrates the scale a global progressive jackpot can reach. It does not prove that a small deposit improves the odds, that a jackpot must fall after a certain period, or that the event can be repeated. The deposit of just over €2 is not evidence of the stake used on the winning spin.

To understand a progressive jackpot, look beyond the large number displayed beside the game. The more useful details are the eligibility conditions, trigger rules, pool funding and the way the jackpot is represented in the RTP and game information. Those details tell a reader more than one spectacular winner story.

## What to check before playing

Read the rules for the exact game version. Check whether the selected stake qualifies for the jackpot, whether specific paylines must be active, how the displayed pool resets after a win and whether the RTP includes the jackpot. Do not rely on an old screenshot or a general game description if the operator offers a different version.

Mega Fortune is used here as a historical and technical case study. This article does not establish that the game is currently available or permitted for every account in Germany. Availability and legal eligibility must be checked for the specific offer and location. Gambling is not a way to earn money. Set a fixed limit, use only money you can afford to lose and do not chase losses.
