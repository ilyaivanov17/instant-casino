This guide groups existing information by topic so rules and limitations are easier to compare. Game rules, RTP figures, bonus terms, payment options and legal availability can vary by version, operator and country. Check details directly in the version currently offered.

### How to find the deposit area

1. Open Instant Casino using the official address you already trust and sign in. Never enter account credentials on an independent information site.
2. In your account, look for **Cashier**, **Deposit**, or a similarly named payment section. The exact label may vary.
3. Choose a method that is actually shown to your account. Check its minimum amount, currency, fees and processing estimate in the cashier.
4. Enter the amount and review the summary. If you want a bonus, read its terms before paying and confirm that your chosen method qualifies.
5. Confirm only after checking the method, amount and fees. Keep the receipt or transaction reference.

### Why a payment may fail

A transaction can be declined because of a card or account limit, mismatched account-holder details, an unsupported currency, a security check or a decision by the payment provider. Recheck the cashier details and contact support through a verified official channel if needed. Do not try to bypass verification by using another person's account or payment instrument.

### Fees, currency conversion and withdrawals

A casino-side statement that no deposit fee is charged does not rule out costs from a bank, card issuer, e-wallet or crypto network. Currency conversion may also affect the final amount. Before depositing, check whether withdrawals must return through the same method and whether identity verification is required.

### Cryptocurrency precautions

For crypto deposits, the asset and network must match the details displayed by the cashier. Sending funds to the wrong address or through an unsupported network may be irreversible. Account for network fees, confirmation requirements and exchange-rate changes, and verify every detail before sending.

### Payment security and personal limits

Use only payment methods you own or are authorised to use. Never share passwords, one-time codes or full card details with an editorial website. Set a fixed entertainment budget before playing and do not deposit to chase losses. Gambling is entertainment, not a source of income.
