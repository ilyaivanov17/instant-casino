## What Megaways changes

In a Megaways game, the number of symbols shown on each reel can vary. That changes the number of possible winning ways from one spin to another. Pragmatic Play describes 5 Lions Megaways as a 7×6 game with up to 117,649 ways to win.

The maximum is a description of the possible reel configuration, not a prediction for a particular spin. It does not mean that every round produces anything close to that number of combinations, and it says nothing by itself about the size or frequency of future wins. When reading the game screen, use the ways count displayed for the current spin rather than treating the maximum as an expected result.

## Tumbles and Gold Lion Wilds

After a winning combination, the symbols involved can disappear and new symbols can fall into the open positions. This tumbling sequence may create further combinations as part of the same play sequence. To understand what happened, follow the full sequence instead of looking only at the first win.

The Gold Lion is the game's Wild symbol. The provider's launch description says it substitutes for regular symbols but not for Scatter symbols. Pragmatic Play lists base-game Wild multipliers ranging from 1× to 40×. Check the current in-game help to see exactly how the multiplier is displayed and applied in the version you are playing.

## Free spins: seven selectable options

Three Scatter symbols trigger the bonus feature. In the original design, the Scatter is represented by a Yin and Yang symbol. Before the free-spins round begins, the player chooses from seven options. The options combine different free-spin counts with different Wild-multiplier profiles. The provider describes a maximum of 25 free spins and multipliers of up to 40×.

This is a choice between bonus configurations, not a way to predict the outcome. Spin count and multiplier range are different characteristics; neither guarantees a particular total win. Before starting, check which options are available in the loaded version and what its rules say about any additional free spins.
