**Bigger Bass Bonanza** by Pragmatic Play combines a fishing theme with a Free Spins feature in which Wild symbols collect visible money symbols. Its defining interaction is between the Scatter trigger, Fisherman Wilds and a multiplier that increases in stages.

## Game layout and confirmed details

| Detail | Information on the official product page |
|---|---|
| Provider | Pragmatic Play |
| Grid | 4×5 layout |
| Scatter trigger | 3, 4 or 5 Scatters |
| Free Spins | 10, 15 or 20, depending on the Scatter count |
| Money Symbols | Values up to 4,000× bet can appear in the bonus game |
| Fisherman Wild | Collects all visible Money Symbols |
| Wild retrigger | Every fourth Wild awards 10 additional Free Spins |
| Multiplier sequence | 2×, then 3×, then 10× |
| RTP, volatility and stake limits | Not stated on the cited product page |

The 4,000× figure refers to the value stated for Money Symbols in the bonus game. It is not automatically the game's maximum total payout. Check the help for the casino version actually loaded for its RTP, volatility and stake limits; the cited official product page does not list those settings.

## How the Free Spins feature starts

Scatter symbols trigger the bonus feature. Pragmatic Play describes three possible trigger combinations and the corresponding starting number of Free Spins:

| Scatters on the reels | Free Spins |
|---:|---:|
| 3 | 10 |
| 4 | 15 |
| 5 | 20 |

So the trigger determines not only whether the feature begins, but also how many spins are awarded at the start. The active version's help explains the applicable stake and payout rules.

## Fisherman Wilds and Money Symbols

During the bonus game, Money Symbols can appear with individual values. Wild symbols perform the collection: a Fisherman Wild collects the values of all visible Money Symbols on the screen.

Two parts of the feature should be kept distinct:

1. **Money Symbols show values:** those values are the amounts that can be included by the collection feature.
2. **Wilds collect the visible values:** the Wild triggers the collection; a Money Symbol being visible does not, by itself, mean its value has been credited.

Pragmatic Play states that Money Symbols in the bonus game can have values up to 4,000× the bet. The value of a particular symbol and the outcome of a round cannot be predicted.

## Additional Free Spins and the multiplier

The collection feature also has a progression. According to the provider, every fourth Wild triggers the feature again: ten additional Free Spins are awarded and the multiplier for collected values moves to the next stage.

The provider describes the multiplier sequence as:

- first retrigger: 2×;
- second retrigger: 3×;
- final stage: 10×.

This explains why the number of collected Wild symbols matters during the bonus. However, another Wild does not guarantee a particular cash amount: the result still depends on which Money Symbols are visible and how the active version settles the collection.

## What the bonus feature does not guarantee

Triggering Free Spins, landing a Fisherman Wild or reaching a higher multiplier does not guarantee a particular payout or a profitable session. The multiplier changes how collected values are evaluated; it does not predict which Money Symbols will appear on later spins.

The official product page explains the core feature but does not publish a specific RTP configuration, volatility category or stake range there. Those figures are therefore not copied from third-party listings.

## What to check before playing

Open the help and paytable for the version that actually loads and check:

- how 3, 4 and 5 Scatters determine the number of Free Spins;
- which symbols count as Money Symbols during the bonus;
- how Fisherman Wild collects visible values;
- when a fourth collected Wild awards extra spins and advances the multiplier;
- which RTP and stake limits apply to your currency and game version.

Also check the full game title. **Bigger Bass Bonanza** is not the same game as **Big Bass Bonanza 1000** or **Big Bass Football Bonanza**; other entries in the series can use different rules and figures.
