Buffalo King Megaways by Pragmatic Play takes the familiar wildlife theme into a Megaways game with six tumbling reels. Its identity is not just the changing reel configuration: after a win, symbols can disappear and new ones fall into place. The bonus feature adds free spins with Wild symbols carrying fixed multiplier values. Two questions therefore matter during the feature: how many bonus symbols trigger it, and which Wild multipliers appear during the free spins?

## Read Megaways and tumbles separately

Megaways describes the ways to win created by the current reel configuration. The number of possible ways can change as reel heights change. When reviewing an individual spin, the displayed configuration is therefore more useful than a maximum figure in promotional copy.

The tumbling mechanic starts after a win: winning symbols are removed and new symbols fall into the open positions. One paid spin can consequently contain several successive evaluations. Follow the full sequence, because another win may form later in the same tumble chain. The active game's help explains the precise order and symbol rules.

## Bonus symbols and free spins

According to the current product page, landing four to six bonus symbols can award a feature with 12 to 22 free spins. The number of symbols therefore affects which bonus configuration is awarded. The provider also lists an additional prize of up to 100x the stake. This is a description of the product's rules, not a prediction for an individual session.

During the free spins, Wild symbols carry multipliers of 2x, 3x or 5x. Landing three or more further bonus symbols during the feature can award another five free spins. The official product page describes repeated retriggers without a fixed limit. Check the in-game help for the version you have opened to confirm that the same rules are enabled in that casino integration.

## How to read the Wild multipliers

A Wild can substitute for regular symbols and take part in a winning combination. Its multiplier changes the value of a qualifying win according to the rules of the current game phase. The historical launch announcement also describes multiplier values being accumulated on individual tumbles. The game display remains the place to confirm which Wilds are part of a winning combination and which multiplier rule applies at that step.

A visible multiplier does not automatically create a win. Nor does 5x tell you how often that value appears or what a later result will be.

## What to check before playing

- **Reel configuration:** Read the Megaways count shown for the spin; do not treat a maximum as a typical result.
- **Tumbles:** Watch which symbols disappear after a win and which new symbols fall into place.
- **Bonus symbols:** Check the trigger conditions and how many free spins the loaded version awards for four, five or six symbols.
- **Wild multipliers:** Confirm how 2x, 3x and 5x are applied when a Wild contributes to a win.
- **Extra free spins:** Check whether three or more bonus symbols during the feature award five more spins and how retriggers work.
- **Version and figures:** RTP, minimum and maximum stakes, and other settings belong to the help for the version actually loaded. This profile does not claim universal values across operators.
