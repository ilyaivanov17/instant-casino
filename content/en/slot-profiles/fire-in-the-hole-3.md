Fire in the Hole 3 by Nolimit City is set in a frozen mine. Its playfield expands during a round: it begins with three active rows and can open up to six rows through successive collapses. This is more than a conventional symbol cascade. Wins, xBomb explosions, Wild Mining and xHole can trigger further collapses, while features buried in ice may be revealed as the round develops.

## Collapsing Mine: how the playfield expands

Each spin begins with three active rows across six reels. After a collapse, another row becomes active; the grid can expand to six rows. In the fully opened 6×6 layout, the provider lists up to 46,656 ways to win. This is the maximum for the expanded grid, not the number of ways available on every spin.

The game rules list four events that can trigger another collapse: a winning combination, an xBomb Wild explosion, the Wild Mining feature or an xHole symbol. Winning symbols are paid and removed, the remaining symbols fall down, and new symbols can enter from above. A single round can therefore contain several successive evaluations.

## xBomb Wild and Wild Mining

An xBomb Wild substitutes for regular symbols but not Bonus symbols. When it explodes, it removes adjacent symbols – Bonus symbols are excluded – and increases the win multiplier for the next collapse by its value. The provider says visible xBomb Wilds explode before the next collapse, except when the Wild Mining feature is triggered instead.

Wild Mining can activate in the base game when three to six matching symbols line up horizontally or vertically without making a winning combination. No xHole or xBomb Wild can be active at the same time. The triggering symbols are removed and Wild symbols are placed in the middle positions of the alignment. Three aligned symbols create one Wild, four create two, five create three and six create four Wilds. The feature can then trigger another collapse.

## xSplit, xHole and features buried in ice

xSplit divides other symbols on its reel, doubling their size or symbol value. Bonus, xSplit and xHole symbols cannot be split in this way. After activation, the xSplit symbols turn into regular symbols.

xHole draws regular paying symbols in from the reels and scatters split symbols into random positions. It leaves an empty position where the xHole appeared, which triggers another collapse. When multiple xHole symbols land, the symbol size can increase more than once.

Ice blocks can conceal additional features: a Wild, xSplit, xHole, Bonus symbol, multipliers from 2× to 100×, or the Max symbol. These contents are inactive while buried. They can be revealed when an exploding xBomb Wild removes the relevant ice block; xSplit can also reveal symbols in affected blocks. The Max symbol occupies a 2×2 area inside the ice. The provider also describes xGod as a standalone feature that can trigger the maximum win. None of these features predicts what will happen on a later spin.

## Lucky Wagon Spins: coins and enhancers

Three, four or five Bonus symbols trigger Lucky Wagon Spins with two, three or four rows open initially. Six Bonus symbols start the feature with four rows open and a Persistent Dwarf guaranteed on the first spin. The feature begins with three spins; whenever a coin lands in the reel area, the counter resets to three.

Enhancers appear above the playfield. Depending on the symbol, they can set or increase coin values, apply multipliers, trigger dynamite or activate a Persistent Dwarf that collects values in the reel area. An xHole can also trigger three Frozen Wagon Spins. During this sub-feature, the existing coins interact with the values shown in the top row; Lucky Wagon Spins then resume.
