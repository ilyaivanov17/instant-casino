**Fruit Party** by Pragmatic Play is a cluster-paying slot on a 7×7 grid. It does not use conventional fixed paylines: matching symbol blocks create wins and trigger tumbles. Symbols in a winning block can carry multipliers, which work differently in the base game and during Free Spins.

## Base game: clusters rather than paylines

Fruit Party uses a 7×7 grid. A winning combination is formed by a block of matching symbols, not by following a fixed payline. After a win, the symbols involved disappear and new symbols tumble into the open spaces. If another winning block appears, it is evaluated and the cascade continues.

Several successive wins can therefore occur within one paid spin. The tumbles are part of that same game round; they are not automatically additional spins.

## Multipliers in the base game

Pragmatic Play's current product page says that each symbol involved in a winning block can randomly carry a 2× multiplier. If more than one symbol in a combination has a multiplier, their values multiply together. The combined multiplier effect can reach the provider's stated ceiling of 256×.

This is different from addition: the multipliers are not simply added as 2+2+2; they multiply according to the described game mechanic. The active version's display shows how a particular round is settled.

## Free Spins and Golden Fruit Scatters

Three or more Scatters trigger the Free Spins feature. Pragmatic Play lists ten Free Spins as the starting award. During the bonus, winning symbols can carry 2× or 4× multipliers. When several symbols in a winning block carry multipliers, their combined effect can build up to the 256× ceiling stated by the provider.

Golden Fruit Scatters therefore have a different role from ordinary paying symbols: they trigger the bonus and determine when Free Spins can be retriggered. The German product page says that 3 to 7 additional Scatters can retrigger the feature with 10 to 14 more Free Spins.

## What the multipliers do not guarantee

A 2× or 4× symbol does not automatically mean that the stake is doubled. The multiplier applies to the winning block or to the calculation of the winning combination. The result depends on which symbols are part of a valid win and how the active version calculates the payout.

Multiple tumbles or a retrigger do not guarantee a particular total win either. Multipliers describe a calculation rule, not the probability of a given outcome.
