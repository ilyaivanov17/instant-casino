Gonzo's Quest by NetEnt is an adventure slot with five reels, three rows and 20 fixed paylines. Its signature feature is Avalanche: winning symbols disappear, new symbols fall into place and may create further wins. Each additional Avalanche in the same spin changes the win multiplier. The Free Fall round uses higher multiplier levels than the base game.

## Avalanche: several outcomes within one spin

Unlike a conventional reel spin, symbols in Gonzo's Quest fall into position. When a payline combination wins, the contributing symbols disappear. The remaining symbols drop down and new symbols fall from above. If this creates another win, another Avalanche follows.

A single paid spin can therefore contain several consecutive evaluations. To understand the result, follow the whole chain: which symbols formed the first line win, which positions became empty and whether the symbols that fell into place created another combination.

## The base-game win multiplier

The multiplier rises with consecutive Avalanches within the same spin. NetEnt lists a maximum of x5 in the base game. An operator help page documents the steps more specifically as x1 for the first Avalanche, x2 for the second, x3 for the third and x5 from the fourth Avalanche onward. That step sequence refers to the documented operator version; check the display in the game you have opened.

When no further winning combination forms, the Avalanche chain ends and the multiplier resets for a new spin. The multiplier is therefore tied to the sequence of wins within one round, not a value that stays saved between spins.

## Wild symbols

Wilds substitute for regular symbols and can help complete paylines. NetEnt describes them as large stone blocks. The provider's product page also says Wilds can substitute for Free Fall Scatters. Check the help for the loaded version to confirm the reels on which Wilds may appear and their exact evaluation rules.

A Wild does not automatically create a win. It must form part of a valid combination under the active payline rules. Also note that only the highest win per payline may be paid.

## Free Falls: ten spins with higher multipliers

Three Free Fall Scatter symbols on reels 1, 2 and 3 trigger the bonus feature, which awards ten Free Falls. An operator help page says the Scatters must appear consecutively from left to right; the feature is triggered independently of the selected payline.

The Avalanche multiplier increases further during Free Falls. The documented operator help lists x3 for the first Avalanche, x6 for the second, x9 for the third and x15 from the fourth Avalanche onward. The multiplier remains linked to the Avalanche sequence within a spin. Additional Free Fall Scatters during the feature can award more free spins.

## What to check before playing

- **Paylines:** Gonzo's Quest uses 20 fixed lines. Check the active line and stake configuration.
- **Avalanche chain:** Follow which symbols disappear and which fall into place until no further win forms.
- **Multiplier:** Check whether you are in the base game or Free Falls; their maximum levels differ.
- **Wild:** Review the substitution rules and whether Wilds can also stand in for Free Fall Scatters in the loaded version.
- **Free Fall trigger:** Confirm the positions and sequence of the three Scatters, as well as the retrigger rules.
- **Game version:** RTP, minimum and maximum stakes, and other settings belong to the help for the version actually loaded. No universal figures are stated here for all operators.

## Assessment

Gonzo's Quest builds its action around one clear loop: a win starts an Avalanche, falling symbols can create further wins, and the multiplier rises during that sequence. Free Falls use the same core mechanic with higher multiplier levels. When comparing it with other cascading slots, pay particular attention to how the Avalanche chain ends and when the multiplier resets.
