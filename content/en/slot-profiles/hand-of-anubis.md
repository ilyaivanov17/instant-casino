Hand of Anubis by Hacksaw Gaming is an Egyptian-themed cluster slot played on a 5×6 grid. Wins are formed by groups of matching symbols rather than traditional fixed paylines. After a hit, symbols can be removed and replaced through cascades. Another central element is the coloured Soul Orbs: Hacksaw Gaming describes them as a way to build additional multipliers in both the classic game and the Bonus Feature.

## Clusters instead of fixed paylines

A cluster slot forms wins from matching groups of symbols on the grid. This differs from a game where symbols must land on predefined paylines from left to right. The exact size and arrangement of a winning group are determined by the game's own rules; they should not be inferred from cluster mechanics in other titles.

When a winning combination appears, the symbols involved can disappear. New symbols then fall into the vacant positions and may create further wins. A single round can therefore contain several successive cascades. To follow a result, examine each stage separately: which group created the win, which symbols were removed, and what changed after the new symbols fell into place.

## Soul Orbs and multiplier building

The official description presents Soul Orbs as coloured guides that help build additional multipliers. The feature is relevant in the classic game and when the Bonus Feature is activated. This makes the Orbs part of the game's mechanics rather than merely a visual effect.

The release summary does not provide a complete step-by-step rule: it does not specify every collection condition or the exact order in which an Orb changes a multiplier or transfers it to a payout. It would therefore be inaccurate to claim that every Orb automatically adds a fixed amount. Check the active game's help to see which symbols are collected, when an Orb counts and how the resulting multiplier is applied to a win.
