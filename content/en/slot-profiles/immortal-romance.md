### Game overview

This profile covers the original Microgaming release of Immortal Romance. The classic version uses five reels and 243 permanently enabled ways to win. Its bonus structure has two separate features: Wild Desire, which can activate at random in the base game, and the Scatter-triggered Chamber of Spins, which offers four character-based Free Spins modes. Immortal Romance II, Remastered and Immortal Romance Mega Moolah are separate editions and are not treated as the same game here.

| Detail | Classic-version rules |
|---|---|
| Grid | Five reels, 243 ways to win |
| Wild multiplier | The logo substitutes for regular symbols and doubles a win it helps complete |
| Scatter | Scatter Knocker wins independently of paylines |
| Wild Desire | Random base-game feature that turns up to five reels into Wild Reels |
| Chamber of Spins | Three or more Scatters trigger one of four character bonuses |
| Free Spins modes | Amber: 10, Troy: 15, Michael: 20 and Sarah: 25 spins |

### The base game and Wild multiplier

All 243 ways are permanently active in the classic ruleset. Regular combinations must run from left to right across adjacent reels. The Immortal Romance logo is a Wild multiplier symbol: it can substitute for regular symbols and doubles the payout of a combination it helps complete. It does not substitute for a Scatter. If the Wild symbol forms its own winning combination, that standalone payout is not doubled again.

### Wild Desire: random Wild Reels

Wild Desire is a separate random feature in the regular game. When it activates, up to five reels are transformed into Wild Reels. Neither the normal Wild multiplier symbol nor Scatter symbols appear during this feature. Wild Desire should therefore not be confused with the Chamber of Spins: it modifies a base-game result, whereas the Chamber is triggered by Scatters and opens a Free Spins choice.

### Chamber of Spins: four characters, four bonus rules

Three or more Scatter Knocker symbols trigger the Chamber of Spins. In the classic ruleset, Amber is available from the start. The other character modes unlock through repeated Chamber entries: Troy after five entries, Michael after ten and Sarah after fifteen. Each mode has its own number of Free Spins and its own added feature.

#### Amber: ten Free Spins with a 5× multiplier

Amber awards ten Free Spins. All wins during this round are multiplied by 5×. The feature can retrigger, and additional spins are added to the remaining count. Amber is the first mode available in the Chamber of Spins.

#### Troy: Vampire Bats and multiplier Wilds

Troy awards 15 Free Spins. When a win occurs, the Vampire Bats feature can transform random symbols into Wild multiplier symbols. Those symbols can apply multipliers of up to 6× to completed wins. The game help for this mode says it cannot retrigger during Free Spins.

#### Michael: Rolling Reels up to 5×

Michael awards 20 Free Spins and introduces Rolling Reels. After a win, the winning symbols are removed and the symbols above them fall into the empty positions. If the shifted symbols create another win, it is evaluated and the multiplier increases by one, up to 5×. If the next shift does not form a win, the multiplier returns to 1×. Scatter combinations do not activate this cascade feature, and the mode cannot retrigger during Free Spins.

#### Sarah: Wild Vine and additional spins

Sarah awards 25 Free Spins. If the Wild Vine symbol appears on reel three, it can transform other regular symbols into Wilds. Scatters can also award additional spins during this mode: two, three, four or five Scatters add one, two, three or four Free Spins respectively. The extra spins are added to the remaining count.

### Progression and version-specific settings

Unlocking the character modes is part of the classic Chamber of Spins structure. The way progress is displayed and some settings may still vary with the supplied game version. RTP is not stated here as a universal number: operator help pages can list different configurations. Check the information screen and paytable in the version actually loaded.

### Our assessment

Immortal Romance combines three layers that should be kept separate: the 243-way base game with its doubling logo Wild, random Wild Desire with up to five Wild Reels, and the Scatter-triggered Chamber of Spins. The four Chamber modes differ in more than their Free Spins count: Amber multiplies wins, Troy adds Vampire Bat Wilds, Michael chains wins through Rolling Reels, and Sarah combines Wild Vine with Scatter retriggers.
