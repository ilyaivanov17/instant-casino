Le Bandit by Hacksaw Gaming is a cluster slot played across six reels and five rows. Its feature sequence goes beyond a standard cascade: winning positions are marked as Golden Squares and can later be activated by a Rainbow symbol. The activated squares reveal coin values or special symbols such as Four-Leaf Clovers and Pots of Gold. The game also has three bonus modes, with three, four or five FS symbols determining the route.

## Super Cascades: winning positions remain relevant

When a win occurs, the provider says the contributing regular paying symbols, along with other visible symbols of the same type, disappear. New symbols then fall into the vacant spaces. This process repeats while further wins continue to form, so a cascade can contain several steps.

The important detail is that the positions matter as well as the symbols. Each position that formed part of a win is marked with a Golden Square after the symbols disappear. Those highlighted cells prepare the board for the next feature. When following a round, keep track of both the new clusters and the positions that have already been marked.

## Golden Squares: Rainbow, coins and special symbols

A Golden Square is activated when a Rainbow symbol appears in the same spin. The marked positions then reveal different contents: Bronze, Silver or Gold Coins, as well as Four-Leaf Clovers or Pots of Gold.

Hacksaw Gaming lists these coin ranges: Bronze from 0.2x to 4x, Silver from 5x to 20x, and Gold up to 500x. Four-Leaf Clovers can multiply adjacent cells; the provider describes values from 2x to 10x. Pots of Gold next to them can also be affected.

Pots of Gold then collect the visible coin values and, where applicable, the values of other Pots of Gold. They store the resulting combined multiplier sum. According to the game description, Pots activate from top to bottom and left to right. After the last Pot in view has collected, Golden Squares can activate once more and the sequence repeats.

This explains why the Rainbow symbol is central to the feature: it activates positions that have already been prepared. A Golden Square on its own is not yet a coin value and does not guarantee a payout.

## Three bonus modes with different triggers

Le Bandit has three bonus modes. The number of FS symbols in the base game determines which mode starts or whether an active bonus can be upgraded.

### Luck of the Bandit

Three FS symbols trigger this mode. Golden Squares remain highlighted between spins until a Rainbow activates them. Retriggers are possible: the provider says two FS symbols award two extra spins, while three award three extra spins. Four FS symbols can upgrade the current bonus to All That Glitters Is Gold. The remaining free spins carry over, with four additional spins added.

### All That Glitters Is Gold

Four FS symbols trigger this mode directly. It starts with twelve free spins. Golden Squares stay highlighted throughout the round and can be activated repeatedly by Rainbow symbols. The provider says its retrigger rules follow those of the previous bonus mode.

### Treasure at the End of the Rainbow

Five FS symbols trigger this mode with twelve free spins. All highlighted Golden Squares remain in place for the entire bonus. A Rainbow symbol is also guaranteed to land on every spin, activating those positions. Hacksaw Gaming explicitly states that this mode cannot be purchased through the Bonus Buy feature.

## What to check in the game

- **Clusters and cascades:** Follow which symbols form a win, which disappear and which positions become Golden Squares.
- **Rainbow:** Check when the symbol appears and which highlighted positions it activates.
- **Coin values:** Distinguish Bronze, Silver and Gold, as well as the separate effects of Four-Leaf Clovers and Pots of Gold.
- **Collection order:** Watch the order in which Pots of Gold collect values and when Golden Squares are activated again.
- **Bonus triggers:** Three, four or five FS symbols lead to different modes. Check the retrigger and upgrade rules as well.
- **Game version:** RTP, minimum and maximum stakes, and other settings must be checked in the help for the version actually loaded.

## Assessment

Le Bandit's distinctive feature is the connection between cluster cascades and Golden Squares. Cascades create new winning positions; Golden Squares preserve those positions for possible activation by a Rainbow. Coins, Clovers and Pots of Gold add further steps to the feature calculation. The three bonus modes differ not only in their free-spin count, but also in how long highlighted positions remain and how often Rainbow can activate them.
