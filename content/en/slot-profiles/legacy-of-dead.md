### At a glance

Legacy of Dead is an ancient-Egyptian video slot from Play'n GO. The provider's official game page gives 2 January 2020 as its release date. Its central feature is the Tomb symbol, which serves as both Wild and Scatter, followed by Free Spins built around a randomly selected Special Expanding Symbol.

| Paylines | Up to 10 selectable lines in that specific game-help configuration |

### The Tomb: one symbol, two roles

The Tomb symbol has two functions. As a Wild, it substitutes for other regular symbols and can help complete winning combinations. As a Scatter, it triggers the Free Spins feature: Play'n GO says that three or more Tomb Scatters unlock the bonus round. The provider page confirms both roles, but it does not publish every setting used by individual casino versions.

### Starting Free Spins and retriggers

The game-help document reviewed for a configuration marked PA and USD lists 8 initial Free Spins. If at least three Scatters land again during the feature, that document awards another 8 Free Spins. Each such retrigger also selects one additional Special Expanding Symbol at random.

The 8-spin starting award and 8-spin retrigger are therefore tied to a particular rules document. They should not be assumed to apply to every market, operator or build without checking its in-game help. Play'n GO's current product page independently confirms that Tomb Scatters can land during Free Spins and that the feature has unlimited Free Spins potential, but it does not state a fixed number of spins there.

### How the Special Expanding Symbol works

At the start of Free Spins, one regular symbol other than the Scatter is chosen at random to become the Special Expanding Symbol. When enough of that symbol lands, the game-help document says it expands to cover all positions on the relevant reel. The document does not specify the exact number required, so this profile does not add a threshold.

The same rules document describes a special win-evaluation rule during Free Spins: combinations made with Special Expanding Symbols may include gaps between matching symbols. This exception applies to those special symbols in the bonus feature; it should not be read as a general rule for every symbol in the base game.

### Paylines and round settings

The reviewed game-help document describes a five-reel game with up to ten selectable paylines. The number of active lines can be chosen in the settings window, where lines are activated in numerical order. For regular line wins, the document refers players to the paytable and describes left-to-right evaluation. Check the paytable and line setting shown in the version actually loaded before starting a round.

### Keep published payout figures separate

Play'n GO's official game page describes potential winnings of up to 250,000 coins. The game-help document reviewed for the PA/USD configuration instead lists a maximum win of 500,000 USD. These are different units and source contexts. Without evidence that both figures refer to the same game build and payout configuration, they cannot responsibly be converted into one common multiplier. This profile therefore does not present a universal maximum-win figure.

The two primary sources reviewed do not state a universally applicable current RTP or volatility label. Neither is added here. The information panel in the game provided by an individual operator is the relevant reference for that loaded version.

### Our analysis

The defining progression in Legacy of Dead is the way its Free Spins can develop: a randomly chosen Expanding Symbol may be joined by further symbols after Scatter retriggers. That changes the structure of the bonus round as it continues. It does not establish how often a retrigger will occur or predict the result of a particular session.

Before playing, check the exact game title, active paylines, Scatter rules and the RTP and stake information shown in the loaded version. Other games in the Dead series may use related imagery or terminology, but their rules should not be treated as interchangeable with Legacy of Dead.
