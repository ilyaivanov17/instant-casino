### Game overview

This profile covers the classic Microgaming Mega Moolah game: a five-reel slot with 25 fixed paylines and a separate progressive-jackpot game. It does not describe later spin-offs that use the Mega Moolah name with different grids or mechanics.

| Detail | Classic-version rules |
|---|---|
| Developer / current jackpot portfolio | Microgaming; the Mega Moolah network is now operated by Games Global |
| Grid | Five reels and 25 paylines |
| Wild | The Lion substitutes for regular symbols and doubles a win it helps complete |
| Scatter | The Monkey symbol |
| Free Spins | Three or more Scatters trigger 15 Free Spins |
| Free Spins multiplier | Regular wins and wins involving the Wild multiplier are tripled during the feature |
| Jackpot game | A separate, randomly triggered wheel with four progressive tiers |

### The Lion Wild: substitution with a 2× effect

The Lion is the game's Wild symbol. It can substitute for regular symbols to complete a winning combination on a payline. When a win is completed with the Lion Wild, its payout is doubled. The Wild does not replace Scatter symbols, so its role is separate from the Free Spins trigger and the jackpot feature.

### Monkey Scatters and 15 Free Spins

Three or more Monkey Scatters trigger the Free Spins feature. The classic game description awards 15 Free Spins whether the trigger contains three, four or five Scatters. During the feature, regular wins are tripled; wins involving the Lion Wild multiplier also receive the Free Spins multiplier. The stake and active paylines follow the triggering game round.

Free Spins can be retriggered, with additional spins added to the remaining count. The jackpot wheel is separate and cannot be triggered during the Free Spins feature. This distinction matters: Free Spins are a game bonus round, while the wheel is a separate random feature linked to the progressive jackpot network.

### The jackpot wheel: Mini, Minor, Major and Mega

Mega Moolah is linked to four progressive jackpot tiers: Mini, Minor, Major and Mega. The jackpot game is triggered separately and at random during the regular game. A wheel divided into coloured segments is then spun. The segments correspond to the jackpot tiers, and the result determines which tier is awarded.

Progressive jackpots are funded through the linked network and change as the game is played. Fixed cash amounts therefore do not belong in a timeless editorial profile: the relevant value is the current amount displayed by the version actually loaded. Eligibility and network variants can also differ by market and operator.

### What this profile does not state universally

RTP, stake limits and live jackpot amounts are not presented as universal values. Historical and operator-specific help pages may describe different configurations. Check the information screen and paytable in the version actually open. A progressive jackpot is a possible award within a separate feature, not a typical payout or a prediction.

### Our assessment

The classic game has three distinct layers: the regular payline game with the doubling Lion Wild, 15 Free Spins with tripled wins, and the independently triggered jackpot wheel. To understand a round, first identify which of these features is active. The four jackpot tiers belong to the linked network; they are not fixed prizes in the ordinary reel paytable.
