Money Train 3 by Relax Gaming continues the steampunk series with its four returning characters: Collector, Sniper, Necromancer and Collector-Payer. The provider's announcement highlights three new symbol features: Persistent Shapeshifter, Absorber and Tommy Gun. The game also includes the Money Cart Bonus Round and a Respins feature that can start after a spin with no win. These mechanics have different roles and should not be treated as one combined bonus.

The details about the new features and their basic operation come from Relax Gaming's official announcement dated 22 September 2022. It describes the product features but does not list every internal trigger or evaluation rule.

## Three new features with different jobs

### Persistent Shapeshifter

According to Relax Gaming, the Persistent Shapeshifter transforms into any feature on each spin. The important question is which feature it takes on in the current round. The announcement does not specify which feature choices are active in every operator version or how each possible transformation is evaluated in detail. Check the symbol description and animation in the loaded game.

### Absorber

The Absorber soaks up all multipliers and adds them to its own value. It therefore does more than change one individual win: it brings multiple multiplier values together in a single symbol. When reviewing the feature, follow which multipliers appear on the reels and when they are collected by the Absorber. Confirm the full sequence and payout in the active game's help.

### Tommy Gun

The provider says the Tommy Gun is held by the Payer or Sniper. It targets one symbol type before adding to that symbol's value. This differs from a general multiplier because the effect is directed at a particular symbol category. Check which symbol is targeted in a given round and how its value changes, using the game animation and help for the loaded version.

## Money Cart Bonus Round

The Money Cart Bonus Round returns from Money Train 2. In Money Train 3, Relax Gaming says it is triggered by landing three or more bonus symbols anywhere on the reels. The symbols that trigger the feature each reveal a value displayed as a multiple of the stake. Once the bonus starts, three spins are awarded.

The announcement describes the trigger and the three spins, but not every detail of the collection or final calculation. Check which values the triggering symbols display, how they are treated during the three spins and how the final result is credited under the rules of the loaded version. The bonus symbols do not need to land on a particular payline according to the provider's announcement.

## Respins after a spin with no win

The Respins feature can trigger at the end of any spin that does not produce a win. One symbol already present on the reels is selected at random and held in place for an additional spin. The game then plays one more spin.

This feature is separate from the Money Cart Bonus: it is not triggered by three bonus symbols, but by a spin with no win. Check which symbol is held and how the extra spin is evaluated under the version you have opened.

## What to check when reviewing a round

- **Feature symbols:** Distinguish Persistent Shapeshifter, Absorber and Tommy Gun; each changes the game in a different way.
- **Absorber:** Follow which multipliers are collected and how they add to the symbol's value.
- **Tommy Gun:** Watch which symbol type is targeted and how its value changes.
- **Money Cart:** Check the three bonus symbols, their stake multiples and the three awarded spins.
- **Respins:** Confirm that the previous spin had no win, identify the randomly held symbol and follow the extra spin.
- **Game version:** RTP, minimum and maximum stakes, and other payout rules must come from the help for the version actually loaded. No universal figures are added here.

## Assessment

Money Train 3 adds more to the series than another Money Cart round. Its three new features have different functions: the Shapeshifter changes form, the Absorber combines multipliers and the Tommy Gun increases the value of a targeted symbol type. The Money Cart Bonus Round and Respins also have different triggers. To review a round clearly, first identify which feature has started, then follow only the rules that apply to it.
