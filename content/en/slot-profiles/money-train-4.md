### Game overview

Money Train 4 by Relax Gaming is a Scatter Pays video slot with a base-game Respin feature and a separate Money Cart bonus round. Its special symbols affect the feature in different ways. The developer describes more than 20 features and publishes a maximum win of 150,000× the stake.

### The base-game Respin feature

The Respin feature is part of the base game. Relax Gaming says that certain symbols can become sticky during a respin, while multipliers may also enter the sequence. This should be separated from the Money Cart bonus: the Respin feature changes the base-game flow, whereas Money Cart is a distinct bonus with its own spins and special symbols.

The exact combination depends on which feature symbols appear. It is therefore more useful to identify each symbol's role than simply to count how many special symbols are visible.

### Money Cart: three spins and 20 possible special symbols

Money Cart is the main bonus round. Selected symbols reveal a value shown as a multiplier of the stake. Once the feature begins, three spins are available. One of 20 different special symbols may land during the round and significantly change how the bonus develops.

Relax Gaming presents Money Cart as a combination of feature effects. A symbol's displayed value therefore does not explain the whole outcome: the special symbol that appears and the effect it applies to the feature also matter.

### Arms Dealer, Unlocker, Upgrader and Reset

The provider highlights four special symbols with distinct functions:

- **Arms Dealer** converts Bonus symbols into feature symbols.
- **Unlocker** opens additional rows and expands the available play area.
- **Upgrader** turns ordinary feature symbols into persistent ones.
- **Reset** refills the spin counter.

These symbols affect different parts of the bonus. They should not be treated as interchangeable multipliers: some change the grid, others change symbol properties or the remaining spin count.

### Bonus-buy availability varies by market

Relax Gaming notes that direct access to the Money Cart bonus is available only in certain jurisdictions. Where that option is offered, the launch announcement says the bonus starts with one guaranteed persistent symbol. This does not mean the feature is available at every operator or in every country.

### Interpreting the published maximum win

Relax Gaming lists a maximum win potential of up to 150,000× the stake. This is the provider's published cap, not a typical payout or an indication of the probability of reaching it. The official product information checked for this profile does not state one universal RTP figure, so no RTP value is added.

### Our assessment

The key distinction is between the Scatter Pays base game, its Respin feature and the separate Money Cart bonus. The named special symbols have defined roles: Arms Dealer converts symbols, Unlocker opens rows, Upgrader makes features persistent and Reset replenishes the counter. To follow the mechanics accurately, track each role separately and check the rules of the version actually loaded.
