Starburst is a space-themed slot from NetEnt built around two recognisable rules: paylines can be evaluated in both directions, and the star-shaped Wild can expand across an entire reel and trigger a Respin. Rather than combining many different bonus modules, the game focuses on this recurring feature.

## How the paylines work

Starburst uses ten paylines. Evolution Games' game description says that these lines can pay from left to right and from right to left. This is the “Win Both Ways” rule: a winning combination does not have to begin at the left edge of the reels.

It does not mean that a stake automatically produces a win. Symbols still need to form a valid combination on an active line. Read the in-game paytable to see which symbol sequences qualify and how the payout is calculated.

## The main feature: Expanding Wilds and Respins

The distinctive star symbol is the Starburst Wild. NetEnt describes the sequence as follows: when the symbol lands, it expands to cover the entire reel. In its expanded form, it substitutes for all regular paying symbols. The expanded Wild also triggers a Respin, giving the remaining reels another chance to land matching symbols.

Three steps help explain the feature:

1. **Identify the Wild:** distinguish the star-shaped Wild from the regular gem, BAR and seven symbols.
2. **Read the expanded reel:** the Wild occupies the full affected reel and can therefore cover several positions on a payline.
3. **Follow the Respin:** the extra spin is part of the feature. It does not guarantee a winning combination or a positive balance.

The feature is easy to recognise, but its outcome still depends on the symbols that land on the other reels. An animation or a triggered Respin does not predict the result of the next spin.

## Understanding RTP, hit frequency and the maximum payout

NetEnt's current Starburst product page lists an RTP of 96.08%, a hit frequency of 23.00% and a maximum payout of 800 times the bet. RTP is a theoretical measure over a very large number of game rounds; it does not predict how much will be returned in one session. Hit frequency is not the probability of making a net profit on an individual spin either.

There is a small difference between the published RTP figures: NetEnt's current product page lists 96.08%, while the Evolution Games page lists 96.09%. Neither figure should be treated as universal without checking the specific game build. For a particular casino version, its own help or information screen is the relevant reference.

NetEnt does not state a volatility category on its current product page. This profile therefore does not independently label Starburst as low, medium or high volatility.

## What to check before playing

Open the rules for the version you are about to use and check:

- that the title is **Starburst**, not another game with a similar name;
- which RTP is displayed for that version;
- the minimum and maximum stakes for your currency;
- how the ten paylines and both-way evaluation are shown;
- the rules for the expanding Wild and Respin in the paytable.

This matters when the same game brand is offered by different operators or in different regions. A figure on a general product page may not exactly match the configuration currently loaded.
