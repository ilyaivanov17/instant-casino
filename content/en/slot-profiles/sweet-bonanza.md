![Sweet Bonanza slot game artwork showing sweets and fruit in a colourful fantasy world.](/images/slots/sweet-bonanza.jpg)

**Sweet Bonanza** by Pragmatic Play is a candy-and-fruit slot on a 6×5 grid. Wins are formed by enough matching symbols anywhere on the grid. After a win, the paying symbols disappear and new symbols tumble into the empty spaces. During Free Spins, multiplier symbols can land; their values are added together at the end of a tumble sequence.

## Base game: matching symbols anywhere

Sweet Bonanza does not use conventional fixed paylines. Pragmatic Play says that 8–12 or more matching symbols anywhere on the grid can form a win. The exact symbol counts and corresponding payouts are shown in the paytable for the active version.

After a win, the relevant symbols disappear. New symbols fall from above into the open positions. If another valid combination forms, it is evaluated and the tumble sequence continues. Several results can therefore occur during one paid spin.

It is useful to distinguish a new tumble from a new stake: the following cascades are part of the same paid game round. They are not automatically additional spins.

## Scatters and the Free Spins feature

The current product page says that four to six Scatters trigger the bonus game with ten Free Spins. Pragmatic Play also lists an award of up to 100× for Scatter events. Check the help in the active version for the exact mapping and display of those values.

The Free Spins feature keeps the tumble mechanic and adds multiplier symbols. During the feature, multiplier symbols with values from 2× to 100× can appear.

## Multipliers: added at the end of a tumble sequence

The multiplier is not simply applied as each symbol appears. Pragmatic Play describes an evaluation at the end of the tumble sequence: the values of all multiplier symbols that appeared during that sequence are added together and then applied to the win.

Illustrative example, not a prediction: if multipliers of 5× and 10× appear during one bonus sequence, their sum is 15×. The actual result depends on which symbols land and how the active version displays its settlement.

## What the mechanics do not guarantee

A long tumble sequence does not promise that the next group of symbols will also win. A multiplier symbol does not predict which values will appear on later spins. The addition rule explains the calculation; it does not establish the probability of a particular result.

The official product page does not give a specific RTP configuration or stake range for every casino version. Those values are therefore not presented here as fixed features. A 2019 Pragmatic Play launch article described the game as high volatility; for a current configuration, check any volatility label in the active game's help.
