**The Dog House** by Pragmatic Play is a dog-themed slot on a 5×3 grid with 20 paylines. Its distinctive feature is Wild symbols with multipliers on reels 2, 3 and 4. In the Free Spins feature, Wilds stay in their positions and can affect combinations on later spins.

## Grid and paylines

The Dog House uses five reels and three rows. The official product page lists 20 paylines. This distinguishes the original game from later titles in the series that use Megaways or cluster mechanics.

A winning combination is evaluated against the active paylines. The in-game paytable shows which symbol sequences qualify and how they are paid. A general description cannot replace that paytable, especially when an operator offers a different build.

## Wild symbols and multipliers

Pragmatic Play describes Wild symbols with multipliers on reels 2, 3 and 4. However, the accessible product page does not specify the exact multiplier values. This profile therefore does not state fixed values such as 2× or 3×.

Two questions matter when reviewing a spin: which reel the Wild lands on and whether it forms part of a valid winning combination. A Wild symbol by itself does not guarantee a win. The rules and paytable of the active version determine how it is evaluated.

## Free Spins: three Bonus Paws and Sticky Wilds

According to the original launch announcement, three Bonus Paw symbols trigger the Free Spins feature. The number of Free Spins is randomly allocated before the feature begins. The announcement does not specify a fixed starting count.

During Free Spins, Wild symbols remain in their positions, according to the product page. This Sticky Wild feature changes the layout for later spins: a Wild that has landed can stay where it is while the other reels spin again. A later combination may therefore use a different set of symbol positions from the previous one.

The available provider texts explain the main feature but do not describe every detail of its settlement. Check the help for the open game to confirm when Wilds remain in place, how wins are counted and whether any restrictions apply.

## What Sticky Wilds do not guarantee

A Wild that stays in place during Free Spins does not guarantee a winning combination. It only changes the symbol layout for later spins. The random allocation of Free Spins does not predict how many of them will produce a win either.

The maximum win of up to 6,750× in the launch announcement is a published figure from 2019. It is not an expected amount or a promise for every game version or operator.
