### Game overview

Wolf Gold by Pragmatic Play has two distinct bonus routes: Free Spins with an oversized symbol across the middle reels, and the Money Respin feature built around money symbols. The second feature can also appear during Free Spins. The details below follow the developer's official game description; individual operator versions may use different settings.

| Detail | Published game information |
|---|---|
| Developer | Pragmatic Play |
| Free Spins | Three Scatters trigger five spins |
| Free Spins feature | Reels 2, 3 and 4 combine into one giant symbol |
| Retrigger | Three more Scatters award three additional spins; the provider states there is no overall retrigger limit |
| Money Respin | Six or more Money Symbols trigger three respins |
| Mega Jackpot | Filling all 15 positions awards the 1,000× Mega Jackpot |

### Free Spins: three Scatters and one giant symbol

Landing three Scatter symbols starts the bonus round with five Free Spins. During this feature, reels 2–4 combine and spin as one giant symbol. The centre of the grid therefore works differently from the base-game arrangement: three separate reels are represented by one oversized symbol.

If three Scatters land again during Free Spins, the feature awards three additional spins. Pragmatic Play describes the retrigger as having no fixed overall limit. This means that another Scatter trigger can extend the feature; it does not indicate how frequently a retrigger will occur.

### Money Respin: money symbols stay in place

The second feature uses a different sequence. Landing at least six Money Symbols triggers Money Respin with three respins. Ordinary symbols disappear, while the Money Symbols that triggered the feature remain visible. The special reels then offer further Money Symbols and empty positions.

Each new Money Symbol stays in place and resets the counter to three respins. The feature ends when the counter runs out or all positions are filled. The values of the visible Money Symbols are then added together and awarded. The provider's description lists Money Symbol values of up to 100× and refers to several jackpot prizes.

### Understanding the 1,000× Mega Jackpot

When all 15 positions in the Money Respin feature are filled, the 1,000× Mega Jackpot is awarded as well. This is a specific completion condition within the feature, not a typical result or a prediction for an individual round.

The official game page also says that Money Respin can be triggered during Free Spins. The two features should therefore be assessed separately: they have different triggers, sequences and end conditions.

### What the product page does not establish

The official product page does not provide one RTP setting or stake range that can be assumed for every operator and jurisdiction. Those figures are not added here. Check the information panel and paytable in the version actually loaded, together with the terms shown by the operator. A jackpot multiplier describes a possible feature award; it does not show the probability of reaching it.

### Our assessment

Wolf Gold stands out because it places two different bonus mechanics side by side. Free Spins change the middle reels and can be extended by Scatters. Money Respin replaces the ordinary symbols with its own arrangement of money symbols and empty spaces. A useful comparison starts by separating these two sequences and checking the rules of the installed game version.
