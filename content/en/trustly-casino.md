Trustly is a **Pay by Bank** payment service: it processes a payment directly from a bank account to a merchant. If you are researching “Trustly casinos”, keep two questions separate: how the payment service works, and whether a particular gambling operator actually offers Trustly and is authorised in your jurisdiction.

This guide explains the general process and the checks to make before depositing or requesting a withdrawal. It does not claim that Trustly is available at Instant Casino or at any particular casino.

## What is Trustly?

Trustly describes itself as a payment service that processes payments between a customer and a merchant. It is neither the customer's bank nor the merchant. You can use it only when the merchant displays Trustly or Pay by Bank as an available payment option.

According to Trustly's help centre, you do not need a separate Trustly account or an additional Trustly app to make a payment. You need an eligible personal bank account and access to the authentication method provided by your bank.

## How does a deposit work?

The process starts on the merchant's own website or app:

1. Open the merchant's cashier or payment section.
2. Select Trustly or Pay by Bank, if it is actually listed.
3. Choose a supported bank and the relevant account.
4. Approve the payment using your bank's security method.
5. Return to the merchant and wait for its payment confirmation.

Do not send money using old bank details or an alleged Trustly account number. Trustly says each payment should be initiated through the merchant's checkout so it can be matched to the correct transaction.

## Can you withdraw with Trustly?

A Trustly integration does not automatically support both deposits and withdrawals. Trustly states that the merchant decides which services are available. A withdrawal must therefore be requested through the merchant's account area. The merchant also decides whether Trustly is available for that withdrawal and when the request is sent.

A Trustly logo on a deposit page is not evidence that withdrawals are supported. Check these points first:

- Is Trustly explicitly listed in the withdrawal section?
- Do the bank-account holder details match the account details required by the merchant?
- What minimums, maximums and fees apply?
- Must identity or payment-method verification be completed first?
- Are there separate conditions for a first withdrawal?

## How fast is Trustly?

There is no reliable universal time estimate. Timing can depend on when the merchant reviews and releases the request, whether it asks for additional documents, and how the banks and payment systems involved process the transfer.

Separate the merchant's internal review time from the subsequent transfer to your bank account. If a payment is delayed, use a verified support channel to request its status and a transaction reference. An advertising phrase such as “instant” is not a guarantee for every bank, account or withdrawal.

## What should players in Germany check?

The availability of a payment service does not establish that a gambling offer is authorised in Germany. The Joint Gambling Authority of the Länder (GGL) publishes an official whitelist of operators and intermediaries holding the relevant authorisation or concession. Search for the **exact website domain** and the relevant gambling category, not just a brand name.

A genuine Trustly payment window does not replace this check. The GGL has also warned about cases in which the involvement of a known payment provider is falsely suggested in connection with unauthorised gambling. Only start payments in the official cashier of an operator whose authorisation you have independently checked.

## Fees, privacy and security

Before approving a payment, check whether the merchant or your bank charges a fee and whether currency conversion applies. Read the privacy information provided by both the merchant and the payment service. Never share passwords, one-time codes or full banking credentials with an editorial website or with someone contacting you outside the official payment flow.

Be especially cautious if someone asks you to send money to a private account or an unfamiliar wallet to “release” a withdrawal. Use only support details that you independently open on the verified website.

## Before using Trustly: checklist

- Trustly is actually displayed in the merchant's cashier.
- The exact domain and gambling category are authorised in Germany, where relevant.
- Your bank account is eligible and authentication takes place through your bank.
- You know whether Trustly is offered for deposits, withdrawals or both.
- Limits, fees, processing stages and possible verification requirements are clear.
- You initiate every payment directly from the merchant's checkout.

Trustly's official guidance says a separate Trustly account is not needed for a payment. Bank authentication is still required.

### Can I withdraw casino funds with Trustly?
Only if the merchant supports Trustly withdrawals. Check the withdrawal section of the account.

### Is Trustly faster than a standard bank transfer?
There is no universal answer. Merchant review, the bank, account status and the payment terms all affect timing.

### Does Trustly mean a casino is licensed?
No. Payment services and gambling authorisation are separate matters. Check the domain against the official GGL whitelist in Germany.
